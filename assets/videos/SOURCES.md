## Vertical demo edits

The source files in `vertical/` are silent 3.8-second portrait crops. Files
ending in `_sequence.mp4` concatenate two or three of those cuts into the
lightweight reels used by the app. The Porto, Lisbon and rail clips come from
the Pexels files above. The additional city clips come from Coverr and are used
only as mood footage for this prototype:

- `rome_city.mp4` — “Old Italian city”:
  https://coverr.co/stock-video-footage/italy
- `rome_vespa.mp4` — “Riding a Vespa scooter on the street”:
  https://coverr.co/stock-video-footage/italy
- `paris_eiffel.mp4` — “The Eiffel Tower in Paris”:
  https://coverr.co/videos/the-eiffel-tower-in-paris-7ktcux4utz
- `paris_cloudy.mp4` — “Eiffel Tower on a cloudy day”:
  https://coverr.co/stock-video-footage/torre-eiffel
- `barcelona_street.mp4` — “Empty streets of Barcelona”:
  https://coverr.co/videos/empty-streets-of-barcelona-lotuubvlfu
- `barcelona_square.mp4` — “A town square in Spain”:
  https://coverr.co/stock-video-footage/barcelona

Coverr license: https://coverr.co/license

The JPG files under `assets/images/travel/` are local still frames extracted
from these same demo clips.
